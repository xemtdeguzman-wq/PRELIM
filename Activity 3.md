# 🌷 PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

## 📌 Project Title

> **Reducing Medication Errors Through a Digital Medication Verification System**

The project proposes a **Digital Medication Verification System** that uses **Barcode-Assisted Medication Administration (BCMA)** integrated with an **Electronic Medication Administration Record (eMAR)**.

The proposed technology provides an additional electronic safety layer that assists nurses in verifying medications before administration while maintaining professional nursing judgment.

---

# 🩷 1. PROJECT CONCEPT

## 💡 What is the Project About?

Medication errors can occur during different stages of the medication-use process, including:

- Prescribing
- Transcribing
- Dispensing
- Preparing
- Administering
- Documenting
- Monitoring

The proposed Digital Medication Verification System uses barcode technology to help verify the:

| 💜 Safety Check | 🩺 Purpose |
|---|---|
| 👤 **Right Patient** | Confirms the medication is intended for the correct patient |
| 💊 **Right Medication** | Confirms the medication being administered |
| ⚖️ **Right Dose** | Helps verify the prescribed dose |
| 💉 **Right Route** | Helps confirm the appropriate administration route |
| 🕐 **Right Time** | Helps verify that the medication is administered at the appropriate time |

> 💜 **Technology provides an additional layer of verification to support nurses in delivering safe patient care.**

# 🚨 2. WHY IS THIS A PROBLEM?

Medication errors remain an important patient-safety concern.

Errors may happen because of:

### 👤 Patient Identification
Incorrect patient identification may result in medication being given to the wrong patient.

### 💊 Medication Selection
A nurse may encounter medications that are incorrectly selected or difficult to distinguish.

### ⚖️ Dose
Incorrect dosage can occur because of unclear orders, calculation problems, or other workflow factors.

### 💉 Route
An incorrect route of administration can compromise patient safety.

### 🕐 Time
Medication may be administered at an inappropriate time.

### 📝 Documentation
Illegible, incomplete, or delayed documentation can affect continuity and medication safety.

### 🔔 Interruptions
Interruptions and heavy workloads may increase opportunities for medication errors.

# 🛡️ THE TECHNOLOGY SOLUTION

## Digital Medication Verification System

### Barcode-Assisted Medication Administration

The proposed system follows a five-step process:

---

### 🟢 STEP 1 — SCAN PATIENT

👤 **The nurse scans the patient's ID wristband barcode.**

The patient's identification is electronically captured before proceeding with medication verification.

⬇️

---

### 🔵 STEP 2 — SCAN MEDICATION

💊 **The nurse scans the medication barcode.**

The medication information is captured by the system.

⬇️

---

### 🟣 STEP 3 — VERIFY

✅ **The system checks the medication against the patient's electronic medication order.**

The system compares the scanned patient and medication information with the electronic order.

⬇️

---

### 🟠 STEP 4 — ALERT

⚠️ **If there is a mismatch, the system provides an alert.**

The alert gives the nurse an opportunity to stop and investigate the potential medication error.

⬇️

---

### 🩷 STEP 5 — DOCUMENT

📝 **If verified, medication administration is documented in the electronic health record/eMAR.**

This supports more consistent and accessible medication documentation.
