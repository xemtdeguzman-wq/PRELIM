## 🌷 About the Activity

> 🎀 **Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles**
>
> This activity focuses on the design principles used to create an effective,
> attractive, and engaging presentation. The goal was to organize visual
> elements in a way that communicates information clearly while maintaining
> an appealing aesthetic.

---

## 🦋 Design Principles Applied

### 💜 01 — Contrast

I used **contrast** by combining dark blue and brown elements with a light cream background. This makes the important information stand out and immediately draws the audience's attention to the **"FREE COFFEE"** title.

### 🌸 02 — Visual Hierarchy

I applied **visual hierarchy** by using different font sizes, weights, and placements. The large **"FREE COFFEE"** heading serves as the main focal point, while the supporting details are presented in smaller text.

### 🪻 03 — Balance

I used **balance** by carefully positioning the university logos, coffee images, title, and event information. The elements are distributed throughout the composition to create a stable and visually pleasing layout.

### 🎀 04 — Alignment

I applied **alignment** by centering the main title and organizing the supporting information neatly. This creates structure and makes the presentation easier for the audience to follow.

### 🦄 05 — Repetition

I used **repetition** through consistent colors, borders, typography, and coffee-related visual elements. Repeating these elements helps create a unified and recognizable design.

### 🌼 06 — Proximity

I applied **proximity** by grouping related information together. The event date, location, and booth information are positioned close to one another, making them easier to read and understand.

### 💟 07 — Consistency

I maintained **consistency** in the colors, typography, spacing, borders, and overall visual style. This gives the presentation a cohesive and polished appearance.

## ☕₊˚⊹ How Did These Principles Improve My Presentation? ⊹˚₊☕

</div>

The design principles improved my presentation by making it **organized, attractive, readable, and engaging**. The use of contrast and visual hierarchy helped emphasize the most important message, while balance and alignment created a clean and organized composition.

The use of repetition, proximity, and consistency helped connect the different visual elements together. These principles allowed the information to be communicated clearly without making the presentation look overcrowded.

Overall, applying these principles helped transform the presentation into a **visually appealing promotional material** that captures attention while effectively communicating the event details.
