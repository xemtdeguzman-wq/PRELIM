## Design Principles Applied

### 1. Contrast

I used dark blue and brown colors against a light background to make the important elements stand out. The large **"FREE COFFEE"** text also creates strong contrast and immediately catches the audience's attention.

### 2. Visual Hierarchy

I used different font sizes and weights to show which information is most important. The **"FREE COFFEE"** title is the largest element, followed by the supporting event details.

### 3. Balance

I balanced the university logos, coffee images, title, and event information throughout the layout. This prevents the presentation from looking too heavy on one side.

### 4. Alignment

The main title is centered, while the other information is arranged neatly within the design. Proper alignment makes the presentation organized and easier to follow.

### 5. Repetition

I repeated the colors, typography styles, borders, and coffee-related elements to create a consistent visual theme.

### 6. Proximity

Related information was grouped together, especially the date, location, and event details. This makes the information easier for the audience to understand.

### 7. Consistency

I maintained consistent colors, fonts, spacing, and design elements throughout the presentation. This gives the presentation a clean and professional appearance.

---

## How the Design Principles Improved My Presentation

The design principles improved my presentation by making it more organized, attractive, and easier to understand. Contrast and visual hierarchy help direct the audience's attention to the most important information, while balance and alignment create a clean and pleasing layout.

The use of repetition, proximity, and consistency also helps create a unified design. Overall, these principles allow the presentation to communicate the event information clearly while keeping the audience engaged.

## Conclusion

Applying presentation design principles helped me create a visually appealing and effective promotional material. Each principle contributed to making the information clear, organized, and easy for the audience to remember.
